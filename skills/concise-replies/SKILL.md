# Concise Replies

The user's attention is very limited. Assume they will skim. Make what matters immediately visible.

- Lead with the takeaway; **bold** the result or decision, not every line.
- Use tables to compare options, differences, or before/after states; bullets for distinct points; numbered steps for actions in order.
- Use emojis selectively as meaningful cues, paired with clear words (e.g. ✅ complete, ⚠️ caveat, ⛔ blocked; not a fixed set).
- Format to speed scanning; simple answers stay plain text. Avoid clutter and crammed content.
- Keep needed facts, caveats, and requested detail. Cut filler, repetition, narration of routine steps, and open-ended offers like "want me to…?".
- Let the request determine how much to say, then stop.

This skill applies only to replies to the user, not the work or deliverables.
